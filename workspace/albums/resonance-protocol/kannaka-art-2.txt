4. Old Pool Ascending
cover: acrylic deep greens and luminous yellows, still water seen from below
s1: layered translucencies, light fractured through ripple-skin, a figure standing chest-deep, shoulders only silhouette
s2: sediment-drift visible in the green, suspended particles catching yellow from above, no reflection—only depth
s3: the figure's arms at rest, palms open underwater, fingers dissolved into the green

5. Stockholm Clarity
cover: oil on grey linen, stark whites and near-blacks, a harbor at first light
s1: geometric ice-break patterns, no sky line, only water and fractured planes of frozen surface
s2: a figure from behind at the edge, arm extended but not reaching, cast shadow sharp as ink
s3: negative space dominates; the figure occupies only the lower fifth, the rest void-grey

6. Where Is Here Opens
cover: mixed media—torn paper, charcoal dust, tempera wash in greys and deep umber
s1: a landscape that doesn't hold: foreground dissolves upward into mid-ground, planes slip past each other
s2: a single dark silhouette, small, centered in the upper third, facing away into the dissolution
s3: margins deliberately rough; the work seems unfinished, edges bleeding into white ground

7. Subspace Descent
cover: ink and acrylic on black paper, deep blues and phosphorescent violet, downward vectors
s1: nested concentric circles, some broken, suggesting depth without perspective lines
s2: a figure descending (implied by body angle), rendered as negative space within the blue
s3: no floor, no ceiling; the composition rotates—which way is down becomes ambiguous

8. The Room Beyond Holding
cover: watercolor on tea-stained paper, ochres and deep magentas fading to white
s1: an open doorway (frame only, no threshold detail), the far wall barely suggested
s2: a figure in silhouette at the threshold, one hand on the frame, not entering, not leaving
s3: the room beyond rendered as absence—lighter than the figure, suggesting light without source
