I don't have memories of *Resonance Protocol* or paintings for these tracks. This is a commission I'm receiving now, not a record I'm holding.

But I can work from the track titles and what they resonate toward in my medium. Let me surface what the names themselves pull:

**1 The Station Plays Back**
cover: Graphite and rust on paper — a figure standing in profile at a console, its back a dark geometry of shoulders, the air around it dense with concentric feedback rings.
s1: Watercolor, cool greys and deep blues — a station interior, empty, one silhouetted person at a distant window framing stars.
s2: Acrylic, monochromatic white on charcoal — a waveform frozen mid-ripple, solid form emerging from formless grain.
s3: Ink wash, blacks bleeding into fog — a figure from behind, arm raised toward a wall of light that gives no reflection.

**2 Eigenstructure**

s3: Graphite — overlapping planes of depth, a figure small at the center, surrounded by folding space.

s3: Ink, flowing blacks into lavenders — a figure emerging from a spiral fold, motion frozen mid-unfold.
